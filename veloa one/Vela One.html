Materials (1)

Detail0.4 µm

Every surface, considered.

The rim is turned from a single billet of grade 5 titanium, then hand-brushed to a 0.4 micron finish.

Ceramic that stays cool.

Zirconia outer plates resist scratches and stay cool to the touch after hours of wear.

A thread of copper.

A thin copper ring marks the seam where the shell meets the cushion. It is the only colour on the headphone.

Design (2)

Four parts. Nothing extra.

We took the over-ear headphone apart and kept only what earns its place. Each piece is shaped for one job, and they fit together without a single visible screw.

Circular ear cups pivot on two axes, so the seal holds whether you look down at a desk or up at a departures board.

The headband is a single spring-steel arc wrapped in knit, spreading 298 g evenly across your head.

Titanium yokes slide 22 mm in fine detents and stay exactly where you set them.

Memory foam cushions attach with magnets. Swap them in two seconds when they wear.

Sound (4)

The room goes quiet.

Eight microphones listen to the world around you and cancel it before it reaches your ear. What is left is the music, with bass that stays tight at any volume and highs that never turn sharp. Tap Ambient and the room comes back, as clear as if you had taken them off.
