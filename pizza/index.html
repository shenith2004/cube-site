Our story

Born in Naples, raised in Brooklyn

Forno Nero started in 2009 with one oven, one recipe and a stubborn belief: pizza should be simple, honest and blisteringly hot. Our founder Marco learned the craft in his nonna's kitchen in Naples. Today his team still stretches every base by hand and feeds our black-tiled oven with beechwood every morning.

  • Dough rested for 48 hours, never rushed
  • Flour, tomatoes & cheese from Campania
  • Vegan, vegetarian & gluten-free friendly
